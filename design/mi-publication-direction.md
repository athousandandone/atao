# MI Publication Direction

## Purpose

MI is the publication within **A Thousand And One**.

It explores the idea that **everything is product**—that software, AI, engineering, food, travel, literature, design, art and music all share the same underlying craft of observing the world, making something, learning from the result and making it better.

The publication should feel like a journal of practice rather than a technology blog.

---

## Editorial Character

The publication should feel:

- calm
- thoughtful
- confident
- understated
- timeless

It should invite reading rather than encourage browsing.

The writing is the primary product.

---

## Visual Identity

The primary identity is **A Thousand And One**.

The **MI** device is an identifier rather than the publication masthead. It is intended for uses such as:

- favicon
- footer mark
- browser tab
- social profile image
- GitHub organisation avatar
- watermark
- application icon

The publication should always feel like part of A Thousand And One rather than a separate brand.

---

## Visual Direction

The current direction combines:

- an editorial publication homepage that also serves as the article index
- a long-form article reading experience designed for extended reading sessions

This direction was established through Claude Design exploration and should be treated as the reference point for future implementation work.

The design should feel closer to an editorial publication than a SaaS product.

Inspirational references include:

- The Gentleman's Journal
- Delayed Gratification
- Craig Mod

These references describe tone rather than appearance and should not be copied directly.

---

## Reading Experience

The publication should comfortably support:

- short observations (approximately 200–500 words)
- standard articles (approximately 800–1,500 words)
- long-form essays (2,000–4,000+ words)

Typography should do most of the work.

The layout should remain elegant whether an article consists of uninterrupted prose or a mixture of:

- quotations
- code
- images
- diagrams
- recipes
- tables
- side notes
- footnotes

---

## Content Philosophy

Content is authored in Markdown and rendered as semantic HTML.

The design should embrace the strengths of the web platform rather than recreate them through custom components.

An article is a document, not an application.

---

## Architectural Principles

The publication is intentionally:

- statically generated
- database-free
- Markdown-first
- semantic HTML
- CSS-driven

No browser JavaScript should be shipped unless a future requirement demonstrably justifies its existence.

Every dependency should earn its place.

---

## Colour & Typography

Primary background:

`#fefbf8`

The A Thousand And One logotype uses **Cormorant Garamond**. Supporting serif typography should sit comfortably within that family.

Typography, spacing and rhythm should establish the visual character of the publication.

---

## Future Evolution

This document records the intended direction, not a frozen implementation.

Layouts, typography and implementation may evolve over time, provided they continue to support the principles described above.
